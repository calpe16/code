The [complete product tour](../docs/PRODUCT_TOUR.md) contains 15 new application renders: 12 desktop views and three phone-width views. Three earlier financial renders remain available below.

The two fixtures are independent; figures need not match between the earlier financial screens and the expanded tour. All captures use the application's real widgets with fictional data in a fresh in-memory store. The complete source, configuration, attached documents and account credentials are not included.

The new gallery was generated by one selected Flutter widget test. It rendered all views without reported widget exceptions. It does not validate every interaction, a physical mobile device, or external services.

The [numbered illustrations and GIF](WALKTHROUGH.md) explain CLI decisions; they are not screenshots or video of a running application.
